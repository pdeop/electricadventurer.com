I'm an environmentalist who is deeply hopeful about clean technology and the EV transition. We are one with nature. We belong to this incredible planet, and going electric is a key step in aligning our mobility with the ecosystems we love.

I drive a Rivian R1S, travel with my wife and our 100 lb golden doodle Korra, and love exploring the Cascades and the Rockies.

## What I Believe

I believe EV road tripping is the ultimate modern adventure. The charging stops are not an inconvenience; they are the structure of the journey! They encourage us to slow down, walk the dog, grab actual food, and arrive at our destination refreshed. 

But I also believe the EV transition still needs better tools. That's why I build [TrailSpark](https://trailspark.me)—a platform that extracts GPS and time metadata from your road trip photos to build location-aware shareable timelines. 

## What I Write About

This site is where I share my EV road trips, sustainability calculations, and learnings about green exploration:

- **EV Road Trips** — Chronological travel logs, route guides, and peak altitude winter crossings.
- **Charging & Infrastructure** — The honest truth about the charging network, preconditioning, and finding backups in rural stretches.
- **Sustainability** — Doing the math on electric miles and carbon offsets.

Join me on the road, drop your photos into [TrailSpark](https://trailspark.me), and let's go!!
